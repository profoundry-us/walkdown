~/shop $ walkdown threads help
Usage: walkdown threads <list|new|show|reply|set|relabel> ...
walkdown threads [list] [--rule <id>] [--all] [--blueprint <id>] [--json]
The open threads, questions and notes, newest first. --all includes the ended ones;
--rule keeps those anchored to one rule.
walkdown threads new --rule <id> | --screen <id> --body <text> [--kind note|question] [--element <sel>]
[--reason <why>] [--option "<label> :: <why>"]... [--as-agent [--said <text>] [--added <text>]] [--blueprint <id>] [--json]
Open a thread on a rule, or on a screen alone (a design request). --body says what was
seen; with --as-agent, --said carries what the person said and --added what the
machine put beside it.
walkdown threads show <id> [--blueprint <id>] [--json]
One thread in full: its anchor, its body and every reply. Changes nothing.
walkdown threads reply <id> <text> [--as-agent [--said <text>] [--added <text>]] [--as-is] [--attach <file>]... [--blueprint <id>] [--json]
Say something on a thread and leave its status as it was. With --as-agent and --said,
the text is a person's words relayed; it is under their name with the machine marked.
walkdown threads set <id> --status <s> | --verify | --reopen | --waive | --option "<label> :: <why>"...
[--reason <text>] [--reply <text>] [--as-agent [--said <text>] [--added <text>]] [--blueprint <id>] [--json]
Change a thread: its status, or the choices a question offers. Transitions are checked
(a note: open → addressed → verified | reopen | waived; a question: open → answered →
incorporated | reopen | waived). Verified and waived need a named person; waiving and
reopening need --reason. --reply lands with the change, or neither lands.
walkdown threads relabel <label|uuid> [--yes] [--blueprint <id>] [--json]
Give the newer of two threads sharing a label (a merge can leave two) the next free
label, keeping the old one as its alias. Asks first; with no terminal, --yes.
The bare noun is `walkdown threads list`.
[exit 0]
